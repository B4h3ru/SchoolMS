🏫 SchoolMS – School Management System

SchoolMS is a school management system designed specifically for primary schools. The project aims to simplify and organize school-related activities through a centralized digital management system.

🎯 Project Overview

Managing primary school activities manually can be time-consuming and difficult. SchoolMS aims to provide a digital solution for organizing school information and improving the management of school operations.

✨ Project Goals

- Digitize primary school management activities.
- Organize and manage school-related information.
- Provide a centralized database for storing school records.
- Improve the efficiency of administrative processes.
